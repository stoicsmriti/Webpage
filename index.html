About

Mindset first, then the mic.

I started as a strategist and found my way to the camera and the microphone. Today I present on TV, voice brands and host shows, and I run a mindset-coaching page on Instagram where I talk about books and modern wisdom.

That background shapes how I work. I read the brief, understand the audience and bring a point of view to the script, so the delivery sounds like a person and not a read.
